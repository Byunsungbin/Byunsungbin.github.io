Director’s Biography

Making films about people at the edges of belonging.

Sungbin BYUN is a South Korean film director and screenwriter. His work explores identity, disability, gender, family, and the emotional lives of people who are often pushed to the margins.

His short films have screened at international festivals, including the Clermont-Ferrand International Short Film Festival and the Busan International Film Festival. His debut feature, Peafowl (2022), continued his exploration of identity and belonging.
